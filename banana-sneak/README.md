# Banana Sneak

A game by Asa, age 6. Play it at https://asagames.github.io/banana-sneak/

You're a bunch of bananas with legs (or an apple, a pineapple, a watermelon, broccoli or a strawberry). Nobody can know.

1. **Sneak** through the grocery store. People shine a yellow light where they're looking. If you move in the yellow light, they get suspicious. Hold **FREEZE** to flop over and look like a real banana.
2. **Hop into a shopping cart** when the shopper is looking at the shelves. They'll wheel you to checkout.
3. **Hold still** while the cashier scans you.
4. At their house, **roll up everything**. Small things first, then bigger things. Get big enough and you eat the whole world, then roll into a bigger one: the grocery store, the street, the town, the mountains, the whole world, outer space, the galaxy and the universe.

## Controls

| | Keyboard | Phone or tablet |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Arrow keys or WASD | Joystick |
| Freeze | Hold Space | Hold the FREEZE button |
| Sound on/off | M | Speaker button |

Once you've reached a world, you can jump straight back to it from the title screen.
