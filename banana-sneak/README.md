# Banana Sneak

A game by Asa, age 6. Play it at https://asagames.github.io/banana-sneak/

You're a bunch of bananas with legs (or an apple, a pineapple, a watermelon, broccoli or a strawberry). Nobody can know.

1. **Sneak** through the grocery store. People shine a yellow light where they're looking. If you move in the yellow light, they get suspicious. Hold **FREEZE** to flop over and look like a real banana.
   Being chased? Drop a **slippery peel** behind you. Anyone who steps on it goes flying.
   Watch out for **Sir Sniffs-a-Lot**, the store dog. Freezing doesn't fool his nose: run, hide in the fruit stand or make him slip. Grab a **silly disguise** (glasses, big nose and mustache) and shoppers barely notice you for 20 seconds.
2. **Hop into a shopping cart** when the shopper is looking at the shelves. They'll wheel you to checkout.
3. **Hold still** while the cashier scans you.
   On the drive home you ride in the shopping bag. Hold FREEZE on the speed bumps or you fly out!
4. At their house, **roll up everything**. Small things first, then bigger things. Get big enough and you eat the whole world, then roll into a bigger one: the grocery store, the street, the town, the mountains, the whole world, outer space, the galaxy and the universe.

## Controls

| | Keyboard | Phone or tablet |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Arrow keys or WASD | Joystick |
| Freeze | Hold Space | Hold the FREEZE button |
| Drop a slippery peel | P | PEEL button |
| Sound on/off | M | Speaker button |

Once you've reached a world, you can jump straight back to it from the title screen.

## Bosses, stickers and hats

- **Bosses.** Every world has a boss, like Giant Grandma, Biscuit the Giant Dog, the Yeti and the Cosmic Cat. While it's bigger than you, it chases you and bonks stuff off your ball. Grow big enough and you can eat it.
- **Sticker book.** Everything you roll up goes in your sticker book. Rare things (only one in the world) and bosses get special stickers. Open it from the title screen.
- **Dance parties.** Roll up a boombox and everybody in the world dances for a while, so nobody runs away.
- **Hats.** Every world you eat unlocks a new hat: party hat, sunglasses, cowboy hat, propeller cap, chef's hat, pirate hat, alien antennas, crown and halo. Your hat stays on when you turn into a ball.

Your stickers, hats and worlds are saved in your browser on this device.
